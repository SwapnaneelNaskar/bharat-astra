## 🌟 Key Architecture & Ecosystem Verticals

### 1. 🛍️ E-Commerce Shopping & Hardware Customizer
- **14 Rich Product Categories:** Mobiles & Tablets, Laptops & Computers, Audio & Wearables, Fine Jewelry & Diamonds, Men's Fashion, Women's Fashion, Shoes & Footwear, Perfumes & Fragrances, Beauty & Cosmetics, Bags & Luggage, Home & Appliances, Groceries, Fitness & Sports, Books & Stationery.
- **Dynamic Spec Selection:** Real-time RAM & Storage variant selector with live price recalculation.
- **100% Unique Verified Images:** Curated high-resolution imagery for all 177+ catalog items.
- **10-Day Easy Return Guarantee:** Item-level returns with instant wallet/source refunds and Section 34 CGST Credit Notes.
- **Delivery Address Verification Guard:** Mandatory profile address check prior to checkout to ensure accurate doorstep delivery.

### 2. ⚖️ Interactive Product Comparison Engine
- Dedicated **Compare Matrix** tab with category-specific filtering.
- Side-by-side specification, pricing, ratings, and instant "Add to Cart" capabilities for up to 4 items simultaneously.

### 3. ✈️ National & International Flight Bookings (68 Gateways)
- **40 Indian Commercial Airports:** Spanning North, South, East, West, Central, North-East, and Andaman Islands.
- **28 Premier International Hubs:** London (LHR/LGW), Dubai, Singapore, Bangkok, New York, San Francisco, Chicago, Toronto, Paris, Frankfurt, Zurich, Tokyo, Sydney, etc.
- **Dynamic Cabin Class Pricing Engine:** Realistic fare scaling across Economy, Premium Economy, Business Class, and First Class Suites.
- **Dynamic Cabin Seat Maps:** Interactive seating grids matching selected travel classes (3-3 Economy, lie-flat Business pods, private First Class suites).

### 4. 🚆 Nationwide Indian Railways Network (All 18 Zones & 62 Stations)
- **62 Major Railway Stations & Junctions:** Complete coverage of all 18 Indian Railway zones.
- **Dynamic Service Generator:** Real-time train generation (Vande Bharat Express, Rajdhani Express, Duronto Express, Shatabdi/Tejas Express, Superfast Express) with realistic departure/arrival timings and live availability (Confirmed/RAC).
- **Interactive Coach Berth Layouts:**
  - Coach H1 (1A First AC): Private 2-berth Coupes & 4-berth Cabins.
  - Coach A1 (2A 2-Tier AC): 6 berths per bay (Lower, Upper, Side Lower, Side Upper — strictly no middle berths).
  - Coach B1 (3A 3-Tier AC): 8 berths per bay (Lower, Middle, Upper, Side Lower, Side Upper).
  - Coach C1 (CC AC Chair Car) & Coach E1 (EC Executive Chair Car).
  - Coach S1 (SL Sleeper Class): 8 berths per bay.

### 5. 🎬 Nationwide Cinema & Blockbuster Movies (305+ Theaters Across 20 Cities)
- **20 Major Indian Metros & Hubs:** Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Indore, Surat, Bhopal, Patna, Nagpur, Visakhapatnam, Coimbatore, Goa.
- **Multiplex & Cinema Chains:** PVR-INOX, Cinepolis, MovieMax, Miraj Cinemas, and premier IMAX/4DX auditoriums.
- **Auditorium Seat Booking:** Interactive curved screen layout with Recliner VIP Lounge, Prime Club, and Classic seating tiers.

### 6. 🏨 Verified Hotels & Stays (380+ Properties Across 10 Cities)
- Authentic, city-specific architectural and resort photography for every destination.
- Multi-room reservations with granular per-room guest allocation (Adults, Children, Bed Preferences).
- Verified contact telephone and email details for all 380 properties.

### 7. 💳 Multi-Option Payment Gateway & VIP Treasury
- **6 Payment Modes:**
  1. **VIP Treasury / Digital Wallet:** 1-click instant settlement.
  2. **UPI Instant Pay:** Dynamic QR code with live countdown timer and GPay/PhonePe shortcuts.
  3. **Credit & Debit Cards:** 3D interactive card visualizer with CVV toggle.
  4. **Net Banking:** Top Indian banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB).
  5. **Easy EMI & PayLater:** Pre-approved zero-cost EMI plans.
  6. **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Doorstep cash or QR verification.
- **Owner VIP Account:** Pre-loaded ₹7.00 Crore demo treasury for owner authentication (`cristiano7@gmail.com`).

### 8. 🧮 18% Statutory GST Engine & Tax Rules Directory
- Real-time tax calculation splitting CGST (9.0%) and SGST (9.0%) on taxable subtotal.
- Fully compliant with Section 31 (Tax Invoices) and Section 34 (Credit Notes) of the CGST Act, 2017.
- Dedicated interactive modal detailing tax rules and HSN/SAC codes across all platforms.
